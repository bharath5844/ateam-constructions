Questions

Things clients ask us.

Do you make Vastu-compliant house plans?

Yes. Every floor plan we draw follows Vastu principles, and we adjust it to your plot size, road side and family needs.

Do you handle approvals?

Yes. We take care of Gram Panchayat and municipal building permissions, so you do not have to run between offices.

Can I get only a plan or only a 3D elevation?

Yes. You can take plans, 3D elevation, structural design or interiors on their own, or choose full construction.

Can I visit one of your ongoing sites?

Yes. Ask us and we will arrange a visit so you can see our work and quality first-hand.
